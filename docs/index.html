FAQ

Why does the app ask for Location? iOS only allows apps to read Wi-Fi information when Location access is granted. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere.

Why are values approximate? iOS reports Wi-Fi strength as a 0–100% value instead of raw dBm. The app converts it to an estimated dBm number so you can compare spots.

How do I cancel my subscription? Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your iPhone.
