A NOTE ON ACCURACY
iOS shares Wi-Fi strength with apps as a percentage. WiFi Heatmap AR converts this into an estimated dBm value so you can compare spots in your home. Results show relative differences between locations, not laboratory measurements. AR scanning requires an iPhone with ARKit and a connected Wi-Fi network; Location access is required by iOS to read Wi-Fi information.
