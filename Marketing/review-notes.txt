WiFi Heatmap AR needs no login. Wi-Fi strength is read with NEHotspotNetwork (Access Wi-Fi Information entitlement), which requires Location permission; the camera is used for the ARKit floor heatmap. The AR scan needs a physical iPhone connected to Wi-Fi and walking around a room. Without AR hardware, the Preview with a sample home button on the AR tab shows a sample result. Monetization: free tier (signal meter, 3 speed tests per day, first AR scan up to 40 tiles, one saved scan). WiFi Heatmap Pro (auto-renewable subscriptions Yearly and Weekly with a 3-day free trial, plus Lifetime non-consumable) is offered on the paywall after onboarding and from Settings > Get WiFi Heatmap Pro. Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings. The speed test connects to Cloudflare's public speed.cloudflare.com endpoint; no personal data is collected.
